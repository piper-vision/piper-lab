Four ways we create space

Thirteen layout options for a full-width services component. Each pins to the screen and steps through the four services as you scroll; every option also has four links to jump straight to a service.

A

Split index

The four service names are the navigation: the active one darkens as you scroll, with a line filling under it. The photo wipes up on the right, the copy rises in underneath.

What we offer
    B

    Full-bleed

    Each service takes the whole screen. A segmented bar along the top (story style) fills as you scroll and doubles as the four links; the title is set huge over the photo.

    C

    Rail

    The services sit side by side as big cards (the current card style, scaled up) and glide sideways as you scroll down. Tabs underneath with a sliding underline jump to each.

    D

    Stacked deck

    Each service card slides up over the last, which sinks back and dims, like a deck being dealt. A numbered index on the left shows where you are and jumps to any card.

      E

      Colour field

      The whole section takes on each service's colour (the square-button colours), with the photo as a big framed card in the middle wiping up between services, the title to its left and the copy to its right. Chips along the bottom switch services.

      F

      Split slot

      Half colour, half photo. As you scroll, the colour panels slide up while the photos slide down, like a slot machine settling on each service. The tabs bottom-left jump to each.

      G

      Accordion

      Four full-height columns in the service colours across the width. The active one opens out to show its photo, title and copy; the others fold to a narrow strip with the name running up it. Click any column to open it.

      H

      Growing window

      On the service's colour, its name is set huge across the screen and the photo grows from a small window in the middle to almost full screen as you scroll through it, then cuts to the next service on its colour.

      I

      Fanned deck

      Four portrait photo cards, each framed in its service colour, fanned slightly like a hand of playing cards on the active service's colour. As you scroll, the active card straightens and comes to the front while the others fan out behind it (the ones you've seen to the left, the ones to come to the right). Click a card or a link to jump to it.

      J

      Centred card

      On a pale wash of the service colour: the heading and View all top left, the big photo card in the middle framed in the service colour, with the previous service as a small square bottom left and the next one top right. All three photos change with a mask wipe as you scroll; the name, copy and Find out more change on the right. Click a small square to jump to that service.

      M

      Card carousel
