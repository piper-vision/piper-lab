03 · Methodology · copy: data sheets, Assess Design Build Assure
How every engagement runs
One methodology across every service: a structured, low-risk path from first look to lasting capability.
01
Assess
We baseline your current posture through health checks, maturity assessments and threat modelling, quantifying risk and setting a prioritised roadmap.
02
Design
We define target-state architectures and operating models aligned to your business and regulatory requirements, so platforms are built right from the outset.
03
Build
We implement production-grade platforms and pipelines through hands-on engineering, automating controls without compromising delivery velocity.
04
Assure
We validate and operationalise what we build through training, exercises and continuous assurance, so the capability stays with your teams.
