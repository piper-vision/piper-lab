Case study · Banking and financial services
Citigroup: de-risking open source at bank scale
We de-risked open source software use and ingestion behind the firewall for one of the largest banks worldwide, designing and implementing an open source ingestion platform for a community of over 40,000 developers.
IndustryBanking and financial services
Developers served40,000+

ServicesSupply chain security, platform engineering
03 · The problem · copy: booth collateral, approved points
The problem
In the bank’s own terms.
The business recognised that the ingestion of millions of packages per day without any control or guardrail represented an unacceptable security and operational risk. Open source was essential to delivery; uncontrolled open source was unacceptable to the regulator and the risk function.
04 · What we did · copy: booth collateral
What we did
Design and implementation, then handover: we do not operate what we build.
A platform that fits the existing stack
Built to integrate with the bank’s existing technology, re-using as much as possible rather than replacing it.
Modular by design
Completely modular to avoid vendor lock-in: individual tools and capabilities can be added or removed as needs evolve.
A sustainable operating model
We designed and deployed, then agreed a target operating model implemented with internal teams: the bank retains operational control.
Extended beyond open source
The platform’s modular nature later allowed it to extend to closed source ingestion without rework.
05 · The outcome · copy: booth collateral
The internal development community can now consume the open and closed source software it needs from internal, trusted repositories.
