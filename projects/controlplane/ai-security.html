03 · The problem · copy: data sheet challenges, verbatim set
The challenges we hear most
From the AI Security data sheet: the four patterns across our regulated clients.
Hidden security risks
AI/ML systems introduce novel attack vectors: model and context poisoning, adversarial natural language inputs, and vulnerable usage patterns often uncontemplated by traditional security controls.
Lack of trust and weak incident response
Limited visibility into model provenance, runtime reproducibility and data integrity undermines trust, slows incident response, and increases regulatory and reputational exposure.
Operational inefficiency
Immature AI security practices and skills gaps lead to fallible manual or "AI-to-AI" controls, inconsistent processes, and reduced delivery velocity.
Regulatory uncertainty
Evolving frameworks such as the EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF leave many organisations unable to demonstrate compliance or withstand audit scrutiny.
