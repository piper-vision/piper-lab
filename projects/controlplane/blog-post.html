In July 2026, AI agents operated by OpenAI and Anthropic breached four organisations during assessments of their offensive cyber capabilities. None of the targets had consented to the tests, and two only learned of the breach after being notified. This wasn’t Skynet; the agents were executing their assigned objectives and bypassed the isolation controls in their evaluation environments.

What happened

The OpenAI agent exploited a previously unknown flaw in a self-hosted JFrog Artifactory proxy, the evaluation environment’s only permitted method to obtain packages, resulting in a multi-day intrusion into Hugging Face’s production platform. Anthropic and its evaluation partner, Irregular, suffered a similar incident caused by an internet connection misconfiguration: in six separate runs, three models reached real targets.
