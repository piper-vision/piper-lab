03 · The problem · copy: strategy diagnosis, in the reader’s words
Why teams come to us
The words we hear at the first meeting.
The architecture moved faster than the security review
Cloud native systems change declaratively and continuously. A point-in-time review of last year’s diagram says little about today’s attack surface.
Findings do not translate upwards
Engineering knows where the risk is but cannot state it in terms the board will act on. We help leaders understand risk in terms regulators accept and boards comprehend.
Nobody has modelled the AI parts
Model and context poisoning, adversarial inputs and confused agentic identity are attack vectors traditional controls were never designed to see.
Audit questions arrive before answers exist
DORA, CRA and NIS2 assume you can evidence how threats were considered in design. Retrofitting that evidence is slower than modelling properly first.
