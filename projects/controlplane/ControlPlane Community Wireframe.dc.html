03 · Where we hold positions · copy: strategy + booth
Where we hold positions
CNCF
Multiple co-chairs of the Linux Foundation’s Technical Advisory Group on Security (CNCF TAG Security). Engaged by CNCF to threat model KubeFlow.
OpenSSF
Contributors to core initiatives including security assessments in the Open Source Security Foundation.
FINOS
Contributors to the AI Governance framework and Common Cloud Controls in the Fintech Open Source Foundation.
OpenUK
Our CEO advises the UK Government (DSIT) and the House of Lords (PICTFOR) pro bono as CISO of OpenUK, the non-profit foundation for open source.
04 · What we maintain · copy: strategy + guidance + history
What we build and maintain
We do not just operationalise cloud native tools; we build and govern them.
Flux CD
We employ the Flux CD maintainers and ship the enterprise distribution of the CNCF-graduated GitOps engine.
OpenBao
Our founding team includes OpenBao’s top contributor and former Technical Steering Committee chair.
Kubesec, Netassert and Simulator
Open source security tooling we released to the community, among the first security testing tools built for Kubernetes.
Supporting maintainers
We have donated tens of thousands of pounds to security-critical open source maintainers.
