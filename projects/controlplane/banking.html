03 · The problem · copy: strategy + booth, banking-specific
The pressures we see inside banks
Audit findings cascade
An MRA or audit finding becomes a remediation programme, consuming engineering capacity that was meant for delivery. Compliance stays manual while obligations multiply.
Open source enters uncontrolled
Millions of packages a day crossing the firewall without provenance or guardrails is an unacceptable security and operational risk, and CRA scrutiny is arriving.
Scale breaks the tooling
Tens of thousands of applications across hundreds of clusters exceed what default configurations and manual processes were designed for.
Specialists are unhireable
The engineers who understand Kubernetes, security, compliance and AI simultaneously are the scarcest resource in the market.
04 · What we do for banks · services mapped to obligations
Where we are engaged
Grouped by the obligation, not the technology.
Regulatory immunity engineering
Policy-as-code, continuous attestation and immutable audit trails that make compliance a continuous output of the system: DORA, CRA, NIS2, SEC and FCA translated into engineering requirements.
Independent security assurance
Threat modelling, assurance reviews and penetration testing of cloud native estates, with findings stated in terms risk committees act on.
Secure open source ingestion
Ingestion platforms with provenance and guardrails, built to integrate with the existing stack and handed to internal teams to operate.
GitOps and secrets at bank scale
