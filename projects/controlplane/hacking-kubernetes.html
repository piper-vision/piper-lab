What is inside
Threat model first, then attack, then harden: the book follows the same order we work in.
Threat modelling
How to see a cluster the way an attacker does: trust boundaries, attack trees and the paths between workloads, control plane and supply chain.
Hacking
Practical attacks against real configurations, from container breakouts to policy bypasses, demonstrated rather than described.
Hardening
What actually holds: controls, policies and architectural decisions ranked by the attacks they stop.
