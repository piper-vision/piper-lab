02 · Hero (split) · copy: company history, founding story
Who we are
Founded on a platform that had to be secure.
ControlPlane began in 2017, inside one of the first government Kubernetes deployments worldwide. Our founder was leading a cloud native transformation for critical national infrastructure at the UK Home Office and saw the gap at first hand: traditional security wasn't designed for a declarative, software-defined world.
The mission has not changed since: keeping people safe on the internet by securing, implementing and up-skilling the organisations that handle sensitive and personally identifiable information.

03 · Timeline · copy: company history, condensed to five moments
How we got here
2017
Born from real-world need. Founded during a cloud native transformation for critical national infrastructure at the UK Home Office — one of the first government Kubernetes deployments worldwide.
2018
Proving the approach, then sharing the tools. A complete cloud native migration for one of the UK's largest media organisations, and the release of Kubesec and Netassert — among the first security testing tools built for Kubernetes — open sourced and adopted across the global community.
2019
Raising the bar across the industry. Regular speaking and training at KubeCon NA, OWASP and DevOpsDays; CTFs at KubeCon; training delivered for O'Reilly and SANS.
2020
Trusted with the standards themselves. Google engaged us to write the official GKE CIS Benchmarks. The Linux Foundation's Financial Services User Group engaged us to threat model Kubernetes itself.
Today
Over 250 years of collective cloud native experience. The same hands-on mindset, now applied across consultancy, training and enterprise products — with security treated as enablement, not impediment.
04 · Principles (3-up) · copy: history + guidance, evidence-first framing
How we work
Hands-on, not advisory-only
We build, break and secure cloud native systems every day. Recommendations come from engineers who have run the same platforms in production.
Open source by instinct
We open sourced our own tooling from the start, employ maintainers of the projects we secure, and have donated tens of thousands of pounds to security-critical open source maintainers.
Security as enablement
Agile security practices combined with infrastructure delivery, underpinned by developer compassion: security that helps teams ship, rather than stopping them.
