05 · FEATURES — compact 3-up benefit grid (was 3 full-height rows)copy adapted from LISTENTO v3 press coverage

Everything a remote session needs

Stream from your DAW

Share your session, not a bounce.

Stream lossless audio directly from your DAW with VST/VST3/AU/AAX plugins, or from the standalone app. Skip exporting, uploading, and waiting for feedback — review changes and make adjustments live, avoiding version confusion and repeated exports.

Studio-grade fidelity

Lossless up to 384kHz — immersive included.

Transmit up to 128 channels of lossless audio at sample rates up to 384kHz, in formats up to 9.1.6 surround including Dolby Atmos. Listeners receive via web link, desktop app or iOS player — no setup on their end.

Built-in comms

Talk, compare and mix — in one place.

Integrated Talkback and Listenback keep two-way communication inside the app. A Monitor Mixer gives full control over levels, pan, mute and metering, the ABCD player compares four mix versions mid-stream, and Guest Pass lets collaborators stream back to you for up to three hours — no login required.

09 · PRODUCT FAQs — evaluation-focused, separate from support FAQs (per client Q3)NEW section

Product FAQs

What DAWs and formats does LISTENTO support?

VST, VST3, AU and AAX plugins — plus a standalone app for macOS 11+ and Windows 10+. Listeners need only a browser link, desktop app or iOS player.

How does the free trial work?

Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. Cancel anytime during the trial and you won't be charged.

What's the difference between Basic and Pro?

Pro adds up to 150 listeners, MIDI streaming, custom web receiver backgrounds and Apple Spatial binaural rendering on iOS.

How does LISTENTO fit into my workflow?

Insert the plugin on your master bus and share a link — collaborators hear your session in real time, no exports or uploads.
